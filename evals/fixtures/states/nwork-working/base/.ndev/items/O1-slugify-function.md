# O1: A slugify function

Status: active
Validity: current
Topics: slugify

## Outcome

`textkit.slugify(text, max_length=None)` turns a title into a lowercase ASCII, hyphen-separated URL slug, and can cap its length.

## Out of scope

- Third-party dependencies.
- Transliteration of non-Latin scripts.
